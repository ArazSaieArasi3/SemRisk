# SemRisk engineering method v1.1 — 2026-10-05

Status: accepted **method/reporting contract**, not a claim that all evaluation or publication stages are finished. This revision retains the actually executed OGCM-RF process and explicitly compares it with SABiO (Falbo, 2014). SABiO is a methodological reference; it is not retroactively named as the process followed from project inception. No claim to the newest SABiOx profile is made.

## Three distinct roles
The scientific design is ontology-engineering design research addressing a semantic representation problem. OGCM-RF organizes source-to-decision-to-artifact traceability and gates. Ontology engineering proceeds iteratively through requirements/CQs, conceptual/foundational decisions, operational design, implementation and testing. VVEAA organizes the interpretation of resulting evidence; it is not the design method itself.

UFO supports category/identity/dependence analysis and OntoUML is the intended conceptual notation. OWL supplies the selected logical commitments, SHACL local completeness constraints, and rules/application logic temporal or operational policies. Relational storage is a projection. A module name, database foreign key, diagram arrow or successful reasoner does not establish foundational meaning by itself.

## Domain orientation
Domain boundaries organize responsibilities and externally owned semantics: twelve portfolio scopes, ten architecture responsibilities and six formal modules are different views. They need not have identical membership or totals. Domain grouping makes extension ownership explicit; it does not assert adoption of all Domain-Driven Design practices. The domain-membership audit records overlap and preserves Method as a separate responsibility.

## Manuscript-ready method paragraph
SemRisk was developed through an iterative ontology-engineering design research process. Structured source analysis and reconciliation informed semantic requirements and competency questions, which guided concept selection, foundational analysis and modular design. The executed process is organized through OGCM-RF and explicitly mapped to SABiO's development and support activities. Conceptual commitments are separated from operational encodings and local data constraints. Verification and task evaluation are tied to versioned artifacts and predeclared expected outcomes, while design-source mappings, constructed application evidence and independent expert judgments are reported according to their actual roles and completion status.

This paragraph is a bounded draft for P15, requiring final numbered citations and tense adjustment to the final evidence bundle. Do not import the earlier papers' systematic-review, full-pattern-compliance or independent-validation claims without SemRisk-specific evidence.
