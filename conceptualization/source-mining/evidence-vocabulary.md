# SemRisk Evidence Vocabulary

## Evidence tier

- `A` — authoritative standard, official ontology artifact, high-quality foundational/reference work, or direct operational source.
- `B` — peer-reviewed applied work, official framework, validated public dataset.
- `C` — preprint, technical report, implementation repository, supplementary evidence.
- `D` — vendor/informal market material; product context only.

## Fact status

- `SOURCE_FACT` — explicitly present in the source.
- `INTERPRETATION` — analyst interpretation of source meaning.
- `INFERENCE` — derived from multiple source facts or formal reasoning.
- `DESIGN_PROPOSAL` — proposed SemRisk construct or decision not yet canonical.

## Mapping-evidence level

- `LEXICAL`
- `STRUCTURAL`
- `SEMANTIC_ONTOLOGICAL`
- `INSTANCE_DATA_SUPPORTED`

Mapping level must be recorded independently of confidence. Label similarity alone never establishes semantic equivalence.
