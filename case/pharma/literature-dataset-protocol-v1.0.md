# Pharmaceutical enterprise-risk literature dataset: protocol v1.0

Status: protocol ready; dataset extraction not yet completed. Owners: P09/#15, source governance #17; semantic mapping after P06. CM-PharmE ver.1 supplies the ecosystem perspective and frozen v1.0.0 bridge; it is not a source of independent risk-event observations. Target a bounded pilot of **6–10 accessible primary sources and 30–50 risk statements**, stopping at the first complete scenario/actor/consequence coverage within the agreed page-independent supplement budget. These are planning targets, not results or minimums to fill by invention.

## Predeclared roles and eligibility
Maintain two source roles: `design_reused` for all sources already mined in SemRisk (including current pharmaceutical comparators and DS-003), and `evaluation_new` only for a source not inspected before the frozen P06 design. Snapshot the existing source register at the freeze and keep search/extraction timestamps. A later new source cannot retroactively validate an earlier design change prompted by it; reclassify the affected evidence. Independent-source status does not imply independent extractor or real-world ground truth.

Search from CM-PharmE's cited ecosystem literature and primary publisher repositories using pharmaceutical enterprise/supply/quality/financial/regulatory/strategic risk combinations. Record exact query, service, date, retrieved count and inclusion decisions. Include accessible primary research with an identifiable pharmaceutical organizational risk statement and a page/section/table locator. Exclude duplicates, statements without a retrievable locator, purely clinical patient risk with no enterprise linkage, and unsupported general advice. Blogs/communities may form a separately labeled grey-literature supplement if needed; they do not replace the primary-source pilot or acquire equal evidence weight.

## Extraction and mapping
Unit: one source-grounded **risk statement**, not a counted real-world incident. Store source ID, short paraphrase, exact locator, enterprise context, actor/subject, potential event, consequence, and source-asserted likelihood/impact only when present. Distinguish absent, not reported, not applicable and deliberately withheld. Record extraction uncertainty and conflicting interpretations. No fabricated probability, score, date, treatment effectiveness or causality; no long copyrighted passages. Deduplicate overlapping reports using source lineage and scenario context, retaining aliases and original provenance.

Keep raw paraphrase/source evidence separate from SemRisk mapping. Map to the frozen core IDs and external CM-PharmE IDs with `exact`, `partial`, `ambiguous`, `unmapped` or `out_of_scope` and rationale; use `exact` only after identity/category review. Do not force every item into existing classes. Freeze a source-level split before mapping: reused sources demonstrate applicability; genuinely unused sources can expose gaps. Log design changes from the latter and exclude them from unchanged-design evaluation claims.

## Quantitative and qualitative acceptance
100% retained statements need a source, stable locator, role and mapping disposition; zero invented numerical risk values; zero duplicate statement IDs; zero invalid frozen ontology IDs. Report counts with source and statement denominators, missingness by field, exact/partial/ambiguous/unmapped rates, and source-level reuse. Independently review all ambiguous/negative mappings plus a preselected sample when a real reviewer is available; without that reviewer label the extraction single-reviewer and do not report agreement. Full coverage is not the goal; unmodeled risk categories are findings.

## Placement, release and dependencies
In VVEAA, application/query outcomes belong primarily to Evaluation; semantic judgments belong to Validation only when actually obtained. Verification covers syntax/constraints; Assurance records provenance and reproducibility; Assessment decides each claim. The paper cites CM-PharmE, the dataset artifact if actually released and the few sources discussed directly; the supplement contains the complete source bibliography and per-record provenance. Do not hide the evidential basis solely behind an uncited website.

Canonical artifacts: source/search register, statement CSV, mapping CSV, data dictionary, license/rights table, RDF instance graph, relational import and validation report. Use stable local IDs now. Prepare Zenodo metadata only after final rights/version decisions; a DOI is not invented, and Kaggle is optional. Private source data and identifiable expert responses are excluded. No publication action or expert contact is performed by this protocol.
