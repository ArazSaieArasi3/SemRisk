# Paper-1 Responsibility Capability Boundary v1.0

**Owner:** Issue #80

SemRisk Paper 1 supports a responsibility-assignment truthmaker and derived Risk Owner relation. Multiple simultaneously valid Risk Responsibility assignments may concern the same governed Risk; therefore co-ownership is permitted.

The model does not impose a unique-owner cardinality.

The following remain outside the demonstrated Paper-1 responsibility capability:
- delegation chains;
- RACI semantics;
- accountable-vs-responsible taxonomies beyond the generic responsibility assignment;
- committee/group decision authority;
- approval authority and segregation-of-duties;
- organizational hierarchy semantics.

The source workflow assignee field from SRC-OP-001 remains workflow/profile metadata and MUST NOT be equated with Risk Owner without an explicit Risk Responsibility assertion.

Future richer responsibility semantics belong in Enterprise/Governance refinements and must preserve the #71 relator/aboutness contract.
