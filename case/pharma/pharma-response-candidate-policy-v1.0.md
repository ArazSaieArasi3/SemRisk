# Pharma Response Candidate Typing Policy v1.0

**Owner:** Issue #103

A qualitative source statement that proposes a mitigation, policy, management need or intervention is **not automatically** a SemRisk Risk Treatment Strategy, Treatment Activity or Control Mechanism.

## Paper-1 decisions
- **Pooled procurement (PCI-004):** source wording supports a *possible mitigation strategy*; it is represented as a proposed `SR-CPT-026 Risk Treatment Strategy`. This does not imply adoption, execution or effectiveness.
- **Supply-chain transparency (PCI-005):** source wording supports a *management need*. Its exact category is context-sensitive (strategy, policy, requirement, capability, information-quality intervention, activity or control). Paper 1 therefore keeps it as a **Pharma response candidate requiring contextual typing**, not a fixed Core treatment instance.

## General rule
PHM-017 remains a semantic-coding choice. Source response wording must be inspected for:
1. intended vs executed;
2. strategy vs plan vs activity vs persistent control;
3. requirement/policy/capability vs treatment;
4. evidence of effectiveness (normally absent in DS-003).

Lexical labels never determine Core treatment category.
