# SemRisk Paper-1 ERM Operational Scope Contract v1.0

**Owner:** Issue #79
**Status:** FROZEN_POLICY

## What 27/27 means
The statement **27/27 SRC-OP-001 fields dispositioned** means that every field in the selected governed Jira-style source schema has an explicit semantic disposition in SemRisk.

It does **not** mean:
- complete enterprise risk management coverage;
- complete risk-register functionality;
- complete governance, assurance or reporting coverage;
- compliance/conformance with ISO 31000, NIST, COSO, OCEG or another framework;
- 100% semantic correctness.

## Demonstrated operational capabilities
Paper 1 demonstrates bounded support for:
- Risk Register / Risk Register Entry;
- scenario description;
- workflow-state history;
- responsibility-derived owner;
- likelihood / impact / inherent / residual assessment results;
- treatment strategy / plan / activity / control separation;
- evidence and provenance;
- bounded indicator/threshold structures;
- temporal reassessment;
- one executable Pharma federation case.

## Not demonstrated as complete Paper-1 capabilities
The selected source schema does not by itself demonstrate:
- objective-to-risk linkage completeness;
- due-date/SLA/escalation governance;
- approval workflows;
- delegation chains / RACI / committee accountability;
- control-design adequacy;
- control operating-effectiveness assessment;
- assurance/test activity and assurance conclusions;
- KRI governance lifecycle and threshold approval;
- portfolio aggregation/concentration;
- systemic/cascading/interdependent risk;
- emerging-risk lifecycle;
- enterprise appetite allocation/cascading;
- comprehensive opportunity/upside-risk management;
- board/reporting/attestation completeness.

## Claim rule
SR-CL02 may state only that the **selected governed operational schema** is fully dispositioned and can be projected without class-per-column translation. Operational mapping coverage is not semantic-validity or ERM-completeness evidence.
