## What the result means

The successor preserves the selected risk/assessment/record distinctions while making a previously implicit limitation observable: a reasoner can accept missing named participants under open-world semantics. The opt-in shape requires explicit participant evidence and rejects aliases, wrong categories and use of the Risk pattern as a participant. Three owner-query checks also show that adding a counterpart does not silently make that counterpart an owner. This strengthens Verification and reproducibility assurance; it is not domain validation, measured utility or proof of complete conceptual correctness.
