# Governance-Term Boundary v1.0

**Owner:** Issue #89

Paper 1 keeps these constructs distinct because frameworks use them differently:

- **Risk Criteria (SR-CPT-044):** criteria used to evaluate significance, acceptability or treatment choices within a method/governance context.
- **Risk Appetite (SR-CPT-045):** framework-specific governance expression concerning the type/amount of risk an organization is prepared to pursue or retain.
- **Risk Tolerance (SR-CPT-046):** framework-specific expression of permitted variation/bounds around objectives or criteria.
- **Threshold (SR-CPT-025):** method/governance value used to classify, trigger or evaluate observations/results.
- **Indicator (SR-CPT-024):** observed/derived information item used for monitoring.

Non-equivalence rules:
- Appetite ≠ Tolerance.
- Tolerance ≠ Threshold by default.
- Indicator/KRI ≠ Threshold.
- Risk Criteria ≠ Risk Appetite.
- A numeric appetite/tolerance/threshold relation requires explicit framework/method evidence.
- No framework-specific definition is promoted as the universal Core meaning.
