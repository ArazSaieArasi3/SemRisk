# Lightweight expert review v1.1

Status: ready-to-bind protocol, **zero experts contacted and zero responses**. This supersedes only the instrument length/target in v1.0, not its honesty/privacy rules. Bind the final P06/P08 candidate commit and exact diagram before inviting reviewers; no unfinished diagram is labeled reviewed.

Target five actual reviewers with coverage of ontology engineering, enterprise risk, pharmaceutical domain and enterprise architecture/operational use; roles can overlap. Report actual qualifications, relationship to the authors and conflicts. The author is not counted as an independent expert. A missing domain or fewer than four completed reviews gives limited qualitative feedback, not a representative aggregate validation claim.

Provide a one-page scope/claim sheet, legible complete OntoUML figure, definitions and one bounded pharmaceutical example, plus optional full artifacts. Aim for a 20–30 minute review, then adjust from actual feedback rather than claim measured completion time. Use 12 criteria retaining all 20 previous subchecks through the crosswalk. Reviewers may answer subchecks separately; any merged criterion uses the worst substantive rating and records the failing subcheck. This avoids an averaged score hiding a critical problem.

Scale: A acceptable, B minor revision, C major revision, D reject, NA not assessable. Ask for reasons/corrections for C/D/NA and confidence separately. Never compute a mean of letter categories. Report per-item A/B/C/D counts, responding denominator and NA counts, then summarize qualitative defects by severity and resolution. Do not claim inter-rater reliability, saturation, statistical representativeness or consensus from the target sample size.

Any unresolved identity/category error, unsupported causal implication or material misleading claim blocks semantic acceptance regardless of counts. Resolve major findings in a decision log, record dissent and re-review changed items against the exact new commit. Keep reviewer identities/contact details private; use pseudonyms only with appropriate consent. No invented expert responses, author-chat comments or machine checks are counted as independent expert evidence.

Interaction boundary: the autonomous work is packet preparation, candidate binding, response-format checks and analysis of actual supplied responses. Sending invitations or contacting experts requires explicit authorization and a verified recipient list. No contact has occurred. A protocol/template is not expert validation completed.
