## Evidence package

The repository and linked peer-reviewed Version 2 study establish that the released package supports the study's analysed data and table of quotes; study instruments include the semi-structured interview guide, participant information leaflet and sample consent form.

Study context:
- 16 opinion leaders / experts;
- interviews Aug 2023–Apr 2024;
- participants cover Europe, South Africa, India and the USA with local/national/global supply-chain roles;
- purposive sampling;
- interviews audio-recorded and transcribed verbatim;
- anonymised transcripts thematically analysed in NVivo 12;
- coding framework created after the first five transcripts and refined iteratively;
- four researchers analysed interviews and reconciled emerging themes;
- data collection continued until thematic saturation.

## Quality/representativeness implications

Strengths:
- explicit DOI version and license;
- documented ethics/consent;
- transparent purposive sampling and analysis workflow;
- multiple analyst involvement;
- thematic saturation stated;
- source provides actor/cause/consequence/mitigation evidence with quote-level traceability in the released analysis materials.

Limitations:
- n=16 purposive expert sample;
- pharmaceutical-industry participation was limited by confidentiality/employment constraints;
- India had only one consenting participant despite attempted recruitment;
- ecological implications did not emerge in interviews;
- qualitative findings cannot support prevalence or population-frequency claims.
