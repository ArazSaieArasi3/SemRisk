## Case question
Can the SemRisk Paper-1 semantic pattern represent a bounded antibiotic-shortage management case while preserving the distinction among risk context, scenario/event, consequence, assessment/evidence, treatment/responsibility, register/workflow artifacts, and externally owned Pharma entities?

## Bounded population/context
- Domain: systemic antibiotic shortage / access / supply-chain management.
- Evidence population: 16 purposively selected experts/opinion leaders with local, national and global roles.
- Data-collection window: Aug 2023–Apr 2024.
- Contexts represented: Europe, South Africa, India and USA.
- Analytical framework: PESTELI plus emergent qualitative coding.

## Exact primary dataset binding
- SemRisk ID: DS-003.
- Repository: ZivaHub / University of Cape Town.
- DOI/PID: 10.25375/uct.29178665.v4.
- Version: v4.
- Posted: v4 2025-07-02 (v1 2025-06-02).
- License: CC BY 4.0.
- Public package byte size: unverified pending version-4 file manifest; current landing displays Download all (1.94 MB), conflicting with historical 18.01 MB.
- Linked study: Responding to and managing antibiotic shortages: a qualitative study with experts and opinion leaders, Version 2.
- Dataset contents documented by the source/study: analysed data/table of quotes plus study instruments including interview guide, participant information leaflet and sample consent form.

The versioned DOI is the dataset identity used by this case. Individual raw file checksums are not currently available through the connected access path; therefore the case does not make file-checksum-dependent or raw-row-completeness claims.

## Evidence role
DS-003 is discovery/design/reconciliation/illustrative case evidence and is not independent validation. It already shaped Pharma shortage concepts and case framing.

## Fitness limits inherited from #42
- purposive n=16 sample;
- limited pharmaceutical-industry participation;
- only one India participant;
- ecological PESTELI theme was not elicited in interviews;
- no prevalence/population-frequency inference;
- participant statements and analyst/study interpretation must remain distinguishable;
- only released/anonymised public material may be used.

## Case semantic path
Antibiotic shortage context → predisposing/source factors → possible shortage scenario → realized shortage event/condition → affected supply capacity/process/enterprise context → consequence → evidence/assessment → treatment/mitigation activity → responsibility → register/workflow representation

## DS-004 disposition
The Chirac et al. 2026 study remains a preferred structured extension because it reports 350 shortage records harmonized to 64 INNs across seven jurisdictions. The authoritative Figshare share locator is known, but the exact dataset DOI/version/license/file manifest/checksums/columns remain unresolved. DS-004 is therefore excluded from #28 evaluated field-mapping denominator and from any release-bound file-level result. It may enter #47–#49 only after exact qualification.

## Nonclaims
This case does not validate all Pharma, all Health, all drug shortages, prevalence, causality, or universal transferability. Mapping coverage is application-fit evidence only.
