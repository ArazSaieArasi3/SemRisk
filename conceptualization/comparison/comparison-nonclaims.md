# #22 Comparison Nonclaim List

- Do not say SemRisk is the first/comprehensive/universal risk ontology.
- Do not say SemRisk is superior to COVER, ROSE, RISKMAN, AIRO, NIST approaches, RisKG, RiskHub or Pharma comparators.
- Do not infer comparator absence from missing documentation; use `unknown` or `not_reported`.
- Do not treat higher concept/check/CQ counts as higher quality.
- Do not call ISO/ICH/NIST alignment compliance, certification or conformance.
- Do not claim generic EA-risk integration, risk-register digitization/KG/RDB, dashboards, ontology+SHACL, formal reasoning/propagation or Pharma QRM ontology as novelty.
- Do not claim the W2 conceptual differences are validated until formal and claim-dependent evaluation passes.
- Do not treat COVER/ROSE narrower scope as a weakness; they answer different foundational/security questions.
- Do not treat Health/AI features missing from SemRisk Paper 1 as deficits when explicitly out of scope.
- Do not claim bounded Pharma transferability unless SR-CL06 survives evaluation and reports failed/unmapped cases.
