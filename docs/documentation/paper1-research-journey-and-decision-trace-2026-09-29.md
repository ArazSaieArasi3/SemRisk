## Why the semantic boundary was drawn

Operational risk registers use the same words for a phenomenon and its record, a possible scenario and a realized event, an assessment and its result, and a risk state and a workflow status. Paper 1 asks whether these identities can be preserved in a formal Core while still supporting one enterprise register projection and one bounded pharmaceutical federation. The [research contract](../../publications/2026-icae/research-contract.md) states SR-RQ1–3 and contribution limits. These are study questions; the presence of a distinction in the candidate does not show that it was absent in prior work.

## One identity's evolution, rather than a new name per step

The source-mining [G1 conflict record](../../conceptualization/source-mining/g1-conflict-register.csv) and [UL mapping](../../conceptualization/ul/language-to-candidate-mapping.csv) preserved the ambiguity of “owner” and “status.” [Core responsibility](../../conceptualization/core/responsibility-ownership-pattern-v0.1.md) and [risk/workflow state](../../conceptualization/core/risk-state-workflow-state-pattern-v0.1.md) then assigned separate identities. The [foundational baseline](../../foundational/e4-foundational-baseline-2026-09-23.md) treats Risk Owner as a role type and Risk Responsibility as a relator; `hasRiskOwner` is derived. [Formal description](../ontology/formal-ontology-description-p1-r2-v0.2.md) and [relational projection](../wiki/pages/Relational-Projection.md) document their implementation boundary. This is a trace of governed changes in interpretation, not proof that an earlier source was wrong or that every external ontology shares the same commitments.
