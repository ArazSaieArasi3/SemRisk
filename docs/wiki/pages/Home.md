# SemRisk — Start Here

> **Status:** P1-R2 candidate / 0.1.0-rc.1. These are source-controlled Wiki drafts. Final scholarly release, public access and citation remain pending #55/#56. The ontology files and governed evidence linked below are authoritative.

SemRisk organizes operational risk knowledge through a Core ontology and bounded modules/profiles. The Paper-1 candidate separates Risk from register records, scenario from realized event, assessment activity from result, and situational Risk State from workflow status. It has bounded formal and application evidence. Real expert semantic validation (#51), independent E11 shortage-domain testing (#31), final claim calibration (#53), assurance (#54) and release (#55) remain open.
