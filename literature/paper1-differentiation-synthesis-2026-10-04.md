# Claim-critical comparison and concise related-work bridge

## Scope and conclusion
The 10-source, eight-axis view is an updated claim-critical supplement to the broader 204-cell registry, not a replacement for its complete discovery coverage. It separates reviewed overlaps from capabilities not assessed. The older W2 registry is historical; use the dated view and E10 claim-unit audit for current statements. Exact source bindings remain in `literature/ontology-artifact-binding-registry.csv` and the comparator profiles.

Neither UFO/OntoUML-based risk modeling, assessment process/result separation, pharmaceutical risk ontologies, OWL/SHACL, nor ontology-informed relational storage is independently novel here. COVER, ROSE, IOF, RISKMAN and RiskHub provide material prior art. SemRisk's defensible contribution is its **bounded integrated artifact and reproducible task evidence**: distinct risk/record and assessment/result identities, separate workflow/risk states, qualified evidence, selected relational query parity, and an external-owner CM-PharmE bridge. This is an artifact contribution, not a demonstrated exclusive capability or superiority claim. The ablation establishes the role of selected distinctions in answering declared questions; it cannot establish innovation by itself.

## Compact manuscript draft (citations to be resolved in P15)
**Risk ontologies.** Foundational approaches such as COVER and ROSE already connect risk and security to UFO/OntoUML. More recent ontology-network proposals reuse these foundations, while IOF Biopharma formalizes quality-risk assessment processes and results. RISKMAN supplies machine-checkable medical-device risk documentation. These contributions preclude a claim that risk formalization or assessment/result separation is new; they motivate an explicit account of which identities and constraints SemRisk adopts and integrates.

**Standards and enterprise architecture.** ISO risk guidance, the NIST IR 8286 series and enterprise-risk frameworks supply complementary terminology and operational expectations. NIST's register and detail schemas are useful field-level design evidence, while ArchiMate risk research establishes prior enterprise-architecture links. SemRisk reports selected, traceable mappings with their limits; source influence, alignment, coverage and conformance are not interchangeable.

**Operationalization and pharmaceutical application.** RiskHub demonstrates ontology-informed relational risk registers, and MedSupplyKG organizes pharmaceutical supply information as a knowledge graph. SemRisk therefore focuses on a governed combination of semantic distinctions, bounded executable query checks and reference-based federation with CM-PharmE ver.1/v1.0.0. The present constructed case is distinct from the planned literature-derived dataset and any future expert evidence.

**Bridge to method.** The resulting research question concerns whether this bounded core can make these distinctions explicit and preserve the information needed by selected enterprise-risk tasks. The method consequently links source analysis to design decisions, formal constraints, controlled query tasks and separately identified application evidence, rather than inferring quality from ontology size or implementation technology.

Primary locators and limits for each paragraph are in `conceptualization/comparison/paper1-claim-critical-comparison-2026-10-04.csv`. No definitive manuscript novelty claim is accepted without review against the final core.
