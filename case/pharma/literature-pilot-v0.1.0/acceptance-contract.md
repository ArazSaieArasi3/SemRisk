# Run 9 extraction acceptance contract

Bound before statement authoring on 2026-10-05. Parent protocol: `../literature-dataset-protocol-v1.0.md`. Semantic freeze: SemRisk `0.2.0-rc.4`, commit `231a82cde3a43ccbafc06afa744ea9df35af7c26`; external bridge CM-PharmE v1.0.0. No ontology modification in this extraction batch.

The unit is a source-local paraphrased risk statement, not an incident, firm, study participant or global unique risk. Use accessible primary research only for the retained pilot; reviews are discovery/context sources. Search starts from the CM-PharmE v1 ecosystem scope and the existing pharmaceutical baseline. Direct citation snowballing from the conference paper cannot be asserted unless its reference list is inspected.

Accept a bounded pilot when every retained statement has an inspected source locator, source role, explicit missing values, evidence kind, mapping disposition and rationale; IDs and source identities are unique; all ontology targets exist in the frozen release; dedup decisions preserve source lineage. Source reuse or uncertain prior exposure excludes independent-validation claims. Newly searched sources may be `evaluation_new` relative to the recorded repository baseline, but are applicability evidence only.

Budget: at most six fully accessible primary sources in this batch. Extract the first manageable set of distinct enterprise concerns needed to exercise financial, regulatory, supplier, operational and information semantics. Original 30–50 row estimate is not a quota. Stop after mapped records, documented gaps and executable checks exist; extend only to close a specified material gap. Access barriers and regional/publication-age bias must remain visible.

Reject publication claims based on record counts alone; reject converting rankings/weights into probabilities, model estimates into observed incidents, general actors into named companies, future controls into implemented controls, or potential consequences into occurred outcomes. Do not add time, severity, causal edges or CM-PharmE equivalences without source and semantic justification.

Required deliverables: source/screening/search records, JSON/CSV statements, separate mappings and dedup audit, codebook, cited RDF information graph, manifest, reproducible validation and negative controls, article/supplement placement note, and explicit P10 native-database handoff. No DOI, permanent license selection, expert contact or completed expert validation in this batch.

Tests: strict schema and cross-reference checks; source-locator completeness; source-role protection; missing-value consistency; frozen ID membership; round-trip JSON/CSV equivalence; deterministic RDF; SHACL artifact/version/content separation; graph query results; rejection of missing provenance, duplicate ID, invalid ontology ID, fabricated observed event, false holdout and invented numeric likelihood. SQL staging may be added against existing DDL, but complete domain projection remains a separate gate.
