# Scope and contributions

> **Status:** P1-R2 candidate / 0.1.0-rc.1. These are source-controlled Wiki drafts. Final scholarly release, public access and citation remain pending #55/#56. The ontology files and governed evidence linked below are authoritative.

Paper 1 studies selected semantic distinctions, operational mapping, and bounded Pharma federation. The nine headline claims have separate evidence roles and ceilings; none is a blanket ontology validity claim. The selected 27/27 Jira fields are dispositions of one schema. The 8/8 SQL↔SPARQL result applies to eight predeclared tasks. Independent transferability is unsupported on the current evidence.

## How the candidate developed

1. **Evidence to boundary.** Source mining and cross-source reconciliation exposed risk vocabulary, conflicts and coverage gaps. The [G1 decision](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/SemRisk/issues/18) permitted a bounded Paper-1 conceptual synthesis with conditions; it did not make missing source families complete.
2. **Boundary to model.** The [G2 architecture decision](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/SemRisk/issues/24) assigned Core and module/profile responsibilities for the selected distinctions. The resulting [P1-R2 candidate manifest](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/SemRisk/blob/b9217100a4342fc61f0fedb19513126028f55370/ontology/p1-r2-candidate-manifest.yaml) names six Turtle modules, SHACL and the local import policy. A formal candidate is not independent domain validation.
3. **Model to executable check.** The [deterministic semantic build](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/SemRisk/issues/44) checks declared candidate inputs. The [relational parity work](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/SemRisk/issues/49) examines selected SQL↔SPARQL tasks and projection losses. Passing those tasks supports the measured scope, not a universal lossless mapping.
4. **Check to claim.** [Evidence and VVEAA](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/SemRisk/wiki/Evidence-and-VVEAA) separates observed checks from claim interpretation. Independent expert review (#51), shortage-domain holdout (#31), final claim calibration (#53), assurance (#54) and scholarly release (#55) remain open. Negative and partial findings remain visible in those gates.

This is the current candidate's research route, not a complete entity-level version lineage. [Release train](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/SemRisk/blob/b9217100a4342fc61f0fedb19513126028f55370/docs/governance/paper1-release-train.md) distinguishes the evidence, conceptual, semantic, executable, evaluated and publication-bound states; a later planning stage is not inherently a stronger scientific result.
