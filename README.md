# SemRisk

**SemRisk** is a research-first, ontology-grounded program for developing a comprehensive but modular semantic foundation for risk knowledge across enterprise and domain-specific settings.

## Current research intent

SemRisk aims to provide:

- a well-founded **risk core** grounded in UFO/gUFO and aligned with COVER rather than redefining foundational risk semantics without justification;
- explicit separation of risk phenomena, risk scenarios, risk records, assessments, assessment results, controls, treatments, observations, evidence and workflow states;
- standards-aligned mappings to ISO 31000/31073, COSO ERM, OCEG GRC, IEC 31010, Open FAIR and domain standards;
- reusable domain profiles, with **Health** as the highest-priority broad domain and **Pharma** as an early specialized profile connected to CM-PharmE;
- semantic bridges to Enterprise Architecture, Newsium and Commentium;
- an eventual Risk Intelligence Platform supporting continuous sensing, evidence integration, reassessment and explainable risk intelligence.

## Publication strategy

### Paper 1 — conference-first

The first publication target is an IEEE-format conference paper for **ICAEA 2026**, centered on an executable, ontology-grounded enterprise risk register / architecture-risk profile with a bounded Health/Pharma demonstration.

The conference paper intentionally does **not** claim the full comprehensive SemRisk vision. It should establish a precise initial contribution that can be evaluated within the conference page limit.

### Paper 2 — comprehensive journal extension

A later journal article will expand SemRisk toward the full ontology network, broader evaluation, cross-domain profiles, temporal/evidence-aware assessment, risk intelligence and application validation.

## Priority domain profile names

Domain profile names intentionally avoid `and` / `&`.

- `Core`
- `Enterprise`
- `Architecture`
- `Governance`
- `Health`
- `Pharma`
- `Security`
- `Finance`
- `SupplyChain`
- `News`
- `AI`
- `Environment`

These names represent research/modeling profiles, not predefined DDD bounded contexts or software services.

## Current gate

**Gate S0 — Contribution Boundary and Alignment Baseline**

Gate S0 passes only when:

1. closest ontologies, standards, frameworks and semantic systems are compared;
2. reuse / align / extend / new-build decisions are explicit;
3. the conference contribution is separated from the longer-term SemRisk program;
4. priority domain profiles and inter-ontology bridges are documented;
5. candidate novelty claims are phrased conservatively and are traceable to evidence.
