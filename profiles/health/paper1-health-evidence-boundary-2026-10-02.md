## Paper-1 limits

Pharma provides the selected case, not a representative sample of Health. Clinical prediction, diagnostic/treatment decisions, patient-level outcome validation, medical-device conformity, digital-health safety and broad population causal modeling are outside Paper 1. Generic evidence/assessment/observation constructs retain their existing Core status; appearing in Health evidence is not a promotion rule. Any new candidate goes through #18 and the architecture impact review before canonical adoption.
